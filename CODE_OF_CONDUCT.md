# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the code, the math, or the claim —
not the person making it.

Pegana publishes verifiable claims about other people's protocols and assets.
That makes disagreement normal and useful: if you think a threshold is wrong, a
verdict is unjustified, or the methodology is unsound, **say so with the
evidence** — a receipt, an alert id, a replay that disagrees. That is the whole
point of this repository, and it is never unwelcome.

What is unwelcome: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and
publishing others' private information.

## Scope

Applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and the
Telegram channel used for reports and feedback.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **rafael@pegana.xyz**, or privately via
[@PeganaWatchBot](https://t.me/PeganaWatchBot). Reports are handled by the
maintainer and kept confidential.

Note this is a *conduct* channel. **Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)** instead — a bug that lets a tampered receipt
verify as clean needs the private-advisory path, not this one.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments and other contributions that
violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
